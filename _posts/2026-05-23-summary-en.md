## [AI memory demand reprices consumer electronics](https://davidoks.blog/p/ai-is-killing-the-cheap-smartphone) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The surge in AI demand for High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) is diverting DRAM wafer capacity, causing shortages of DDR and LPDDR memory used in PCs and smartphones, and leading to rising prices for consumer electronics. This repricing affects the affordability of everyday devices like laptops and phones, while highlighting the growing tension between AI infrastructure investments and consumer hardware costs. It also underscores the strategic importance of memory manufacturing capacity. The article notes that building a modern DRAM fab costs $15-20 billion, and it takes years to achieve profitable yields. HBM uses 3D-stacked DRAM dies, which require more wafer area per gigabyte than conventional DRAM.

hackernews · d0ks · May 21, 21:55 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48229319)

**Background**: DRAM is a type of volatile memory used in computers and phones. HBM is a high-bandwidth, 3D-stacked variant used in AI accelerators. Because HBM and other DRAM types share the same wafer fabrication processes, increased HBM production reduces capacity for DDR and LPDDR, driving up prices.
