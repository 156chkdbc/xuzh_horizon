## [Scientists Directly Observe Kelvin-Helmholtz Instability on the Sun](https://nso.edu/press-release/nsf-inouye-solar-telescope-enables-major-discovery-of-a-hidden-solar-process/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Using the NSF Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope combined with computer simulations, an international team of scientists has directly observed Kelvin-Helmholtz instability (KHI) on the Sun's surface for the first time. This confirms a process theorized for decades as key to energy dissipation in solar plasma. KHI is believed to be critical for understanding how energy dissipates in solar plasma, which in turn affects the formation of sunspots and flares. This first direct observational evidence opens new avenues for studying solar dynamics, space weather prediction, and plasma physics in general. The observations were made with the world's largest solar telescope, the NSF Inouye Solar Telescope near the summit of Maui's Haleakalā, and supported by computer simulations from an international team including NSO, NSF NCAR High Altitude Observatory, and the Max Planck Institute for Solar System Research. The findings were published in Nature, with images in the paper said to resemble fractal patterns.

hackernews · neversaydie · Aug 5, 15:33 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184355)

**Background**: The Kelvin-Helmholtz instability is a fluid instability that occurs when there is velocity shear within a continuous fluid or a velocity difference across the interface between two fluids. It is visible in cloud formations on Earth and in Jupiter's Red Spot, and was thought to play a role in the Sun's atmosphere. The Inouye Solar Telescope, with its 4-meter mirror, provides unprecedented high-resolution views of the Sun, enabling detection of small-scale (~100 km and below) turbulent features that were previously inaccessible.

## [Chinese-Led BESIII Team Confirms First Glueball, New Form of Matter](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The BESIII collaboration, led by Chinese scientists at the Institute of High Energy Physics, has confirmed the existence of glueballs after 15 years of research. The particle X(2370), first discovered in 2011, was found to have quantum properties consistent with a glueball in 2024, and further decay studies confirmed its flavor-singlet nature, proving it is predominantly composed of glueballs. This is the first experimental confirmation of glueballs, a state of matter predicted by the Standard Model of particle physics but never observed before. It validates a key prediction of the Standard Model and opens a new window for studying the strong force, which binds quarks into protons and neutrons. The BESIII experiment operates on the Beijing Electron Positron Collider (BEPCII). X(2370) was discovered in J/ψ decays in 2011; researchers measured its flavor-singlet quantum state and found multiple new decay modes, distinguishing it from ordinary mesons. Glueballs are extremely difficult to identify because they mix with ordinary meson states.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 6, 07:31

**Background**: In particle physics, a glueball (also called gluonium) is a hypothetical composite particle consisting solely of gluons, without valence quarks. Gluons carry color charge and interact with each other via the strong force. The Standard Model predicts glueballs, but they had never been definitively observed in experiments before this announcement.
